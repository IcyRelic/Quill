# Critique (final round, self-scored)

The skill's critique.md rubric was not read in full; this follows its intent: judge only the screenshots.

What works
- First screen says what this is in five seconds: "Quill, explained step by step", a search field and one button.
- The nib cut is the only shape on the site: hero field, images, buttons, step markers, callouts, code, pager,
  sidebar marker, footer mark. It reads as a system, not decoration.
- Every section carries full content; neighbouring sections differ (picture+list, colour field with four columns,
  heading+picture beside accordion, text beside picture, dark footer with the name at full width).
- Docs pages: Mintlify-like three columns, clean hierarchy, steps and tabs legible at 390px.

Still short
1. The hero leaves empty ground under the button on tall desktop screens (centred column). Acceptable rest, one per page.
2. Doc pages carry no pictures; the sample content has no real Quill screenshots. Replace with real ones via Files.
3. Search is the only live thing; there's no "was this helpful" feedback because there's no backend for it.

Scores (rough, out of 10): design 7.5, usability 8, content 7.5, creativity 7.
