Each `###` heading inside a steps block starts a new numbered step. Everything under it, including code and callouts, belongs to that step.

:::steps
### Open Settings
Choose **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar.

### Turn on sync
Choose **Sync everything**. The first sync can take a minute.

### Check it worked
A tick appears next to **Last synced**.
:::
