---
title: "Accordions"
description: "Questions with answers that open in place."
updated: "2026-10-28T12:00:00.000Z"
---

An accordion keeps a long list of questions short. The question shows, and the answer opens when it is chosen.

:::accordion[Can readers search inside answers?]
Yes. Search reads the whole page, including closed answers.
:::

:::accordion[Can an answer hold other blocks?]
Yes. Lists, code, images and callouts all work inside an answer.
:::

```markdown
:::accordion[The question]
The answer.
:::
```
