A callout sets a few lines apart from the text around it. Use one per section at most, or they stop standing out.

:::note
A note adds context that helps but isn't essential.
:::

:::tip
A tip shows a quicker or better way to do something.
:::

:::warning
A warning says what could go wrong, before it does.
:::

:::danger[Your own title]
Use danger for anything that could lose someone's work.
:::

```markdown
:::tip
A tip shows a quicker way.
:::

:::danger[Your own title]
Text of the warning.
:::
```
