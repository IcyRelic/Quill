Site settings change the parts of the site that aren't pages.

| Setting | Where it shows |
| --- | --- |
| Name and tag | Beside the logo, in the browser tab and in `llms.txt`. |
| Description | Search engines and `llms.txt`. |
| Top bar links | Next to the logo. The link that matches the reader's page is highlighted. |
| Footer | The copyright line (`{year}` becomes the current year), footer links, and the Powered by Quill credit. |

The home page and the site's look have screens of their own.

## Home page

The heading, the sentence under it, whether the faint drawing shows behind them, and the cards.

## Appearance

The logo, the browser tab icon and the colours. Quill's colours are the defaults; change any of them and the tints used for highlights and borders follow. **Reset** puts one colour back, and **Reset all colours to defaults** puts them all back. A preview shows the colours before you save.

## Home page cards

Each card has a title, a short description, a link and a drawing. Choose one of the built-in drawings, or upload a picture to use instead.
