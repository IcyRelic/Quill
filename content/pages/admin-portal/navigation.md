title: "Navigation"
description: "Arrange sidebar groups, pages and dropdowns."
updated: "2026-10-28T12:00:00.000Z"
---

The sidebar is a list of groups. Each group holds pages, and can hold dropdowns that fold a set of related pages under one name.

:::steps
### Add a group
Choose **Add group** and give it a name. Groups appear in the sidebar in the order shown.

### Add pages
Pages that aren't in the sidebar are listed on the right. Choose a group for each one.

### Make a dropdown
Choose **Add dropdown** in a group and name it, then send pages to it from their menu. A dropdown opens by itself when the reader is on one of its pages.

### Save
Choose **Save navigation**. The sidebar updates for readers straight away.
:::

:::note
A page can be in only one place. Sending it somewhere new takes it out of its old spot.
:::
