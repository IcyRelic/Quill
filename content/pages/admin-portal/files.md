---
title: "Files"
description: "Upload and manage images, video and PDFs."
updated: "2026-10-28T12:00:00.000Z"
---

The **Files** screen lists every upload with its size and address. Copy the address to paste it into a page, or delete the file.

Files with the same name are kept side by side: a second `diagram.png` is saved as `diagram-2.png`.

:::warning
Deleting a file does not change pages that use it. Search for its address first.
:::
